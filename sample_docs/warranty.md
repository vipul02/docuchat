# Product Warranty

Sample document for Northwind Outfitters, a fictional outdoor gear retailer used to demo DocuChat.

## Coverage
All Northwind-brand products carry a 2-year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Northwind-brand backpacks and tents carry a lifetime warranty on seams and frames.

## What is covered
The warranty covers zipper failures, broken buckles, delaminating waterproof coatings, seam separation and frame breakage that happen during normal use.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from accidents or misuse, fading from UV exposure, and products modified or repaired by a third party are not covered. Third-party brands follow their own manufacturer warranty.

## How to make a claim
Submit a claim through the Warranty page with your order number and photos of the defect. Our repair team responds within 3 business days. If a repair isn't possible, we replace the product or issue store credit for its current value.
