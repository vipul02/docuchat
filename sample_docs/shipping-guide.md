# Shipping Guide

Sample document for Northwind Outfitters, a fictional outdoor gear retailer used to demo DocuChat.

## Delivery options
Standard shipping takes 4 to 6 business days and is free on orders over $75; below that it costs $6.95. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $19.95. Same-day delivery is available in Seattle, Portland and Denver for orders placed before 12 pm local time.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the UK, the EU and Australia. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Customers pay any import duties and taxes on delivery.

## Order tracking
A tracking link is emailed as soon as the order leaves the warehouse. Customers can also track orders from the Orders page. If tracking has not updated for 5 business days, support opens a courier investigation.

## Damaged or lost parcels
Report damaged parcels within 7 days of delivery with a photo of the item and packaging. Lost parcels are replaced or refunded once the courier confirms the loss, usually within 10 business days.
