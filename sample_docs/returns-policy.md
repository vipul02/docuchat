# Returns and Refunds Policy

Sample document for Northwind Outfitters, a fictional outdoor gear retailer used to demo DocuChat.

## Return window
Online orders can be returned within 30 days of delivery. In-store purchases can be returned within 45 days of purchase. Items must be unused, with original tags and packaging. Members of the Northwind Trail Club get an extended 60-day return window on all orders.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, personalised or engraved products, opened camping fuel canisters and used climbing harnesses cannot be returned for safety and hygiene reasons.

## How refunds are paid
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after the warehouse inspects the item. Orders paid with a gift card are refunded as store credit. Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is caused by our error, such as a wrong or damaged item.

## Exchanges
Size and colour exchanges are free for online orders. Start an exchange from the Orders page; the replacement ships as soon as the return is scanned by the courier.
