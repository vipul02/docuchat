# Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

Sample document for Northwind Outfitters, a fictional outdoor gear retailer used to demo DocuChat.

## Paid time off
Full-time employees get 18 days of paid vacation per year in their first two years, rising to 22 days from year three. Vacation accrues monthly and up to 5 unused days roll over into the next year. Employees also get 10 paid public holidays and 2 "trail days" to spend outdoors.

## Sick leave
Employees get 8 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is only needed for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Remote work
Office and support staff can work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Warehouse and retail roles are on-site.

## Employee discount
All employees get 40% off Northwind-brand products and 20% off third-party brands, for up to $3,000 of purchases per year.

## Onboarding
New hires complete a 5-day onboarding program covering products, safety and systems. Each new hire is paired with a buddy for their first 60 days.
